The story

Five days

Ren is a student whose ordinary days change when the signs start to add up. A bandage Hana explains away with a small smile. A pause at the school gate before she heads home. A silence that gets a little heavier every afternoon.

At home, things have become unbearable, and every adult who should step in looks the other way. Across five rain-soaked days, from Monday's uneasy quiet to Friday's deciding hour, you are the only person she lets close enough to see it.

You cannot fix everything overnight. Sometimes the most you can do is stand beside someone so they do not have to face the dark alone.
